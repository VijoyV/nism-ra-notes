Here are the key formulas used in the NISM Research Analyst Certification material, organized by chapter with brief explanations.

## **Chapter 2: Introduction to Securities Market**
*   **Basis:** $\text{Spot Price} - \text{Futures Price}$. It measures the relationship between the cash and derivatives markets.
*   **Cost of Carry (Commodity Futures):** $F = S + [S \times r \times (t/12)]$. This estimates the fair value of a futures contract by adding the cost of storage, insurance, and financing to the spot price.

## **Chapter 3: Terminology in Equity and Debt Markets**
*   **Equity Share Capital:** $\text{Number of Shares Issued} \times \text{Face Value}$. Represents the nominal value of ownership capital.
*   **Book Value Per Share:** $\text{Net Worth} / \text{Number of Outstanding Shares}$. The theoretical amount each share would receive if the company wound up.
*   **Market Capitalization:** $\text{Market Price Per Share} \times \text{Total Outstanding Shares}$. The total value the market places on a company's equity.
*   **Enterprise Value (Standalone):** $[\text{Market Value of Equity} + \text{Market Value of Debt}] - \text{Cash and Equivalents}$. Reflects the total cost to acquire the entire business regardless of capital structure.
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** $\text{Net Profit} / \text{Number of Shares Outstanding}$. Indicates the profit earned for every issued share.
*   **Dividend Payout Ratio:** $\text{Dividend Per Share} / \text{Earnings Per Share}$. Shows the percentage of earnings distributed to shareholders.
*   **Price to Earnings (PE) Ratio:** $\text{Market Price Per Share} / \text{EPS}$. Measures how much the market pays for every rupee of earnings.
*   **Price to Sales (P/S) Ratio:** $\text{Market Capitalization} / \text{Annual Net Sales}$. Useful for valuing companies with temporary losses.
*   **Price to Book Value (P/BV) Ratio:** $\text{Current Market Price} / \text{Book Value Per Share}$. Compares market price to the accounting value of assets.
*   **Current Yield (Bonds):** $\text{Annual Coupon} / \text{Current Market Price}$. A simple measure of a bond's annual income return.
*   **Holding Period Return (HPR):** $[\text{Coupon} + \text{Reinvestment Income} + (\text{Sale Price} - \text{Purchase Price})] / \text{Purchase Price}$. The total return earned while an investor held a security.
*   **Modified Duration:** $\text{Macaulay’s Duration} / (1 + y)$. Measures the sensitivity of a bond’s price to a 1% change in interest rates.

## **Chapter 5: Economic Analysis**
*   **Per Capita Income:** $\text{National Income} / \text{Total Population}$. A measure of the average standard of living in a country.

## **Chapter 8: Company Analysis – Financial Analysis**
*   **EBITDA Margin:** $\text{EBITDA} / \text{Net Sales}$. Evaluates operational efficiency by excluding non-cash and financing costs.
*   **PAT Margin:** $\text{Profit After Tax} / \text{Net Sales}$. Measures the final residual profit left for shareholders.
*   **Return on Equity (ROE):** $\text{Net Profit} / \text{Average Net Worth}$. Shows how efficiently management uses shareholder capital.
*   **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):** $\text{EBIT} / \text{Average Capital Employed}$. Measures return generated on both debt and equity.
*   **Debt/Equity (D/E) Ratio:** $\text{Total Adjusted Debt} / \text{Net Worth}$. Assesses the financial leverage and risk level of a company.
*   **Interest Coverage Ratio:** $\text{EBIT} / \text{Interest Expense}$. Determines how easily a company can pay interest on outstanding debt.
*   **Current Ratio:** $\text{Current Assets} / \text{Current Liabilities}$. Evaluates a firm's ability to meet short-term obligations.
*   **Quick Ratio:** $(\text{Current Assets} - \text{Inventories}) / \text{Current Liabilities}$. A stricter test of liquidity focusing on immediate cash conversion.
*   **Dupont Analysis (ROE):** $\text{Net Profit Margin} \times \text{Asset Turnover} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}$. Decomposes ROE to identify whether profit, efficiency, or leverage is driving returns.

## **Chapter 10: Valuation Principles**
*   **Gordon Growth Model:** $P = D1 / (k - g)$. Values a company based on a perpetual stream of dividends growing at a constant rate.
*   **Cost of Equity (CAPM):** $Ke = Rf + \beta \times (Rm - Rf)$. Calculates the required rate of return based on risk-free rates and market risk premiums.
*   **WACC:** $[Ke \times We] + [Kd \times (1 - \text{Tax}) \times Wd]$. The average cost of all capital sources used by a firm.
*   **PEG Ratio:** $\text{PE Ratio} / \text{Growth Rate}$. Adjusts the PE ratio to factor in the company’s expected earnings growth.

## **Chapter 11: Fundamental Analysis of Commodities**
*   **Hedge Ratio:** $\text{Correlation} \times (\text{SD of Spot Price Change} / \text{SD of Futures Price Change})$. Determines the optimal portion of a position to hedge using derivatives.

## **Chapter 12: Fundamentals of Risk and Return**
*   **Return on Investment (ROI):** $(\text{Net Profit} / \text{Investment}) \times 100$. A basic measure of profitability for a single period.
*   **CAGR:** $[(\text{End Value} / \text{Beginning Value}) ^ {(1 / n)}] - 1$. The smoothened annual growth rate of an investment over multiple years.
*   **Sharpe Ratio:** $(\text{Portfolio Return} - \text{Risk-Free Rate}) / \text{Standard Deviation}$. Measures excess return earned per unit of total risk.
*   **Treynor Ratio:** $(\text{Portfolio Return} - \text{Risk-Free Rate}) / \text{Beta}$. Measures excess return earned per unit of systematic risk.
*   **Jensen’s Alpha:** $\text{Portfolio Return} - [\text{Risk-Free Rate} + \text{Beta} \times \text{Market Risk Premium}]$. Quantifies the value added by a manager above the market return.
