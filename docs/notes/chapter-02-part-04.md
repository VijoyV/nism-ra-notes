### 2.4.1 Market Intermediaries

| Intermediary | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Stock Exchanges** | Provide trading platform (NSE, BSE, MSEI). Electronic anonymous order matching. Appoint clearing agencies and clearing banks. |
| **Depositories** | Hold securities in electronic form (NSDL, CDSL). Provide services related to demat transactions. |
| **Depository Participant (DP)** | Agent of depository. Interfaces with investors. Holds investor-level accounts. |
| **Trading Members (Stock Brokers)** | Registered members of stock exchange. Facilitate buy/sell transactions. |
| **Authorised Person (AP)** | Appointed by stockbroker as agent to reach investors (replaced sub-brokers from April 2019). |
| **Custodians** | Hold funds/securities of large clients (banks, insurance, FPI). Settle transactions, track corporate actions. |
| **Clearing Corporation** | Legal counterparty to all trades. Guarantees settlement. |
| **Clearing Banks** | Maintain accounts for clearing members. Pay-in and pay-out of funds. |
| **Merchant Bankers** | Issue managers, investment bankers, lead managers. Manage new issue process. |
| **Underwriters** | Subscribe to any portion of public offer not bought by investors. Hard vs soft underwriting. |
| **Farmer Producer Organizations (FPO)** | Collective of farmers to improve bargaining power, market access, economic wellbeing. |

---

### 2.4.2 Institutional Participants

| Participant | Role/Definition |
|-------------|----------------|
| **FPIs (Foreign Portfolio Investors)** | Entities outside India registered with SEBI to invest in India. |
| **P-Note Participants** | Instruments issued by SEBI-registered FPIs to overseas investors not registered with SEBI. |
| **Mutual Funds** | Professionally managed collective investment scheme. Pools money to purchase securities. |
| **Insurance Companies** | Life and general insurance. Major investors in equity, G-sec, bonds. |
| **Pension Funds** | Pool of retirement funds. Provide stable long-term growth. |
| **Venture Capital Funds** | Invest in early-stage enterprises with long-term growth potential. Bring managerial/technical expertise. |
| **Private Equity Firms** | Funding for growth, expansion, or buy-outs (includes venture capital). |
| **Hedge Funds** | Wide mandate across assets, products, geographies. Not necessarily hedged. |
| **AIFs (Alternative Investment Funds)** | Category I (start-ups, SMEs, infra), Category II (real estate, PE, no leverage), Category III (hedge funds, use leverage). |
| **Investment Advisers** | Help investors with asset allocation, financial planning, goal setting. |
| **Warehouse Service Providers** | Storage for commodities. Issue negotiable warehouse receipts (NWRs). Registered with WDRA. |
| **Quality Assayers** | Evaluate and certify quality of commodities. Required for NWRs. |
| **EPF (Employee Provident Fund)** | Defined benefit scheme. Mandatory contributions (12% employer + 12% employee). |
| **NPS (National Pension Scheme)** | Government-sponsored retirement scheme. Subscriber chooses funds. |
| **Family Offices** | Handle wealth, investments, estate planning, tax planning for wealthy families. |
| **Corporate Treasuries** | Temporarily invest surplus funds of companies. |

---

### 2.4.3 Retail Participants

- Individual investors buying/selling for personal account (not for another organization).
- **HNI** = High Net-worth Individuals
- **UHNI** = Ultra High Net-worth Individuals
- NRIs, PIOs, QFIs permitted under Automatic Route (RBI).

---

### 2.4.4 Corporates

- Processors, manufacturers, importers, exporters.
- Dependent on commodities.
- Business margins affected by commodity price variations.
- Procure raw materials → convert to value-added products.

---

### 2.4.5 Proxy Advisory services firms

- Advise investors on exercise of their rights in the company.
- Recommendations on public offer or voting on agenda items.
- Analyze voting proposals and how they affect investor interest.
- Typically engaged by institutional investors.
