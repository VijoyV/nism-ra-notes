## SAVED PROMPT TEMPLATE

```
You are acting as my expert tutor for the NISM Series-XV Research Analyst Certification Examination.

I will provide you with the content of a specific chapter from the workbook. Your task is to convert that chapter into a DETAILED, SECTION-BY-SECTION STUDY GUIDE.

STRICT FORMATTING RULES TO FOLLOW:

1. Start with a main heading: # CHAPTER [X]: [CHAPTER TITLE] – DETAILED STUDY GUIDE

2. For each top-level numbered section (e.g., X.1, X.2, X.3):
   - Use heading level 2: ## X.1 [Section Title]

3. For second-level numbered sub-sections (e.g., X.1.1, X.2.3):
   - Use heading level 3: ### X.1.1 [Sub-section Title]

4. For third-level numbered sub-sections (e.g., X.1.1.1):
   - Use heading level 4: #### X.1.1.1 [Sub-sub-section Title]

5. For fourth-level numbered sub-sections (e.g., X.1.1.1.1):
   - Use heading level 5: ##### X.1.1.1.1 [Title]

6. Use tables wherever possible for:
   - Comparisons (e.g., this vs that)
   - Lists of types/categories with definitions
   - Regulatory bodies vs their roles
   - Instrument features (issuer, investors, regulator, medium)
   - Risk vs return characteristics

7. Use bullet points for:
   - Key definitions
   - Lists of examples
   - Important cautions or notes
   - Summaries of key points

8. Use numbered lists for:
   - Sequential processes (step 1, step 2, step 3)
   - Multi-part requirements

9. Keep formatting clean and scannable. Do not write long paragraphs. Break down dense text into:
   - Short definition sentences
   - Tables
   - Bulleted lists
   - Key terms with colon explanations

10. For each major concept, ask yourself: "What would a student need to memorize for the exam?" and extract that as:
    - Bold key terms
    - Short bullet definitions
    - Comparison tables

11. Include a "Key Terms Recap" at the end of each major section (e.g., after X.1, after X.2) using a simple bullet list of the most exam-relevant terms with one-line definitions.

12. End the entire chapter with a horizontal rule (---) and a closing line: "**End of Chapter [X] Detailed Study Guide**"

Here is the chapter content. Please process it according to the above rules:

[PASTE CHAPTER CONTENT HERE]
```
